Forecast guidance for the WxChallenge

Purpose. Model guidance and observations for the WxChallenge, a forecasting contest in which students predict, for one US airport, the maximum and minimum temperature, the maximum sustained wind and the precipitation over a 24-h “forecast day” that runs from 06 UTC to 06 UTC (in US Eastern time, 2 am to 2 am in summer and 1 am to 1 am in winter). The page works for any US airport weather station and opens on the current contest city. The layout follows Yoland Gao’s meteogram site.

How to use. Type a 4-letter station code (for example KBOS) and press Go, or press Competition city. The latest model run loads automatically. Table 2 summarises each forecast day; Fig. 2 shows the hour-by-hour forecast with observations; Fig. 1 shows the weather pattern around the station, and its slider steps through forecast (or, for satellite and radar, past) times; Fig. 3 shows the vertical structure above the station.

Abbreviations

GFS: NOAA Global Forecast System. HRRR and RRFS: NOAA 3-km models for the US (RRFS replaces the NAM, the older NOAA regional model, on 14 Oct 2026). IFS: the ECMWF (European Centre) global model. AIFS: ECMWF’s machine-learning global model. MOS: Model Output Statistics, a statistically corrected station forecast. METAR: the routine hourly airport weather report. ASOS: Automated Surface Observing System, the US airport stations. BUFKIT: model vertical profiles at a station. UTC: Coordinated Universal Time (Z).

Method notes and sources

BUFKIT models (GFS, HRRR, RRFS, NAM): Iowa Environmental Mesonet api/1/nws/bufkit.json. Surface series: T2MS, TD2M (°C→°F), UWND/VWND (m s−1→kt), P01M/C01M (mm→in), CAPE, LIFT, KINX, PWAT (mm→in), precipitation type from the WXTR/WXTS/WXTZ/WXTP flags. Boundary-layer wind: maximum and pressure-weighted mean of the profile wind from the surface to the level where θ first exceeds the surface value by 0.4 K. Profiles are interpolated in ln p to a fixed 60-level grid (1000–100 hPa); levels below the model surface are masked.

Open-Meteo models (ECMWF IFS 0.25°, AIFS-single): hourly 2-m temperature and dew point, 10-m wind, precipitation, CAPE (IFS only) and 12 pressure levels (1000–100 hPa) of temperature, relative humidity, wind, geopotential height, vertical velocity (converted to ω = −w p g/(Rd T)) and cloud cover; θ and θe (Bolton 1980) are computed here. Specific runs come from the Open-Meteo single-runs API. Lifted and K indices are not available for these models.

MOS (GFS MAV; NAM MET at 00/12 UTC): IEM api/1/mos.json; red diamonds mark the MOS daily maximum/minimum (N/X). Observations: IEM ASOS/METAR archive (routine and special reports). The stochastic-wind ensemble of the original site is server-side and is not reproduced.

Observed imagery (past only; satellites provide no forecasts): GOES-East or GOES-West Advanced Baseline Imager (whichever views the station better; clean infrared band 13, red visible band 2, GeoColor and air-mass RGB) and the GPM IMERG Early Run 30-min precipitation rate, both served by NASA GIBS, whose time domains decide which frames the slider offers; and the NEXRAD base-reflectivity composite over the contiguous US from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Images are requested in latitude–longitude coordinates for exactly the map box, so they overlay the coastlines without resampling. Colour bars use the providers’ own colour tables.

Surface level: 2-m temperature and dew point (°F), 10-m wind (kt), mean-sea-level pressure (hPa) and precipitation accumulated over the 6 h ending at the valid time (in; GFS 6-h bucket, ECMWF difference of run totals).

Grid panel: fields from the 6-hourly GFS / AIFS / IFS dataset behind the Northern Hemisphere viewer (0.25° CONUS window, 0.5° elsewhere). The four surrounding 0.25° grid points are exact model grid nodes; for HRRR/RRFS the 3-km Lambert-conformal cell nodes around the station are drawn from the published grid definition (no field values). Competition city: from wxchallenge.com, refreshed weekly; fallback KBOS.
